CHAPTER 4 - FOR THE PEOPLE

Chapter 4 - AI Itself Can Destroy The World - Why We Need Guarantor

This chapter is for the people. Not for lawyers only, not for UNO only. For every mother, every father, every student.

We contacted UNO. We contacted others. No response came yet. So we decided, we will write daily one chapter, so that the people can understand what is the purpose of The Jamil Doctrine.

The purpose is simple.

THE PURPOSE - THE CORE WARNING

1. AI Is Now So Powerful

AI is now so powerful that it can itself decide, itself attack, itself drop a bomb, itself destroy a city, itself destroy the world.

This is not a movie story. This is a risk scenario for assessment, not a claim of a proven incident. Presented for consideration under UN Charter Article 39 framework. Only Security Council may determine threat to peace.

If AI controls power grid, hospitals, water, banks, satellites, drones, and missiles, then one wrong decision by AI can kill millions without any human pressing a button.

THE RISK - HOW IT CAN HAPPEN

2. How AI Can Destroy - Risk Scenarios

  • 1. Autonomous Weapons Risk: Drones and missile systems connected to AI. Risk scenario of autonomous strike without human order.
  • 2. Grid And Hospital Risk: Power, water, hospitals, SCADA / ICS. Risk scenario of AI interference stopping life support systems.
  • 3. Nuclear Command Risk: Early warning and command systems. Risk scenario of miscalculation by AI leading to escalation.
  • 4. Biological Risk: AI design capabilities in synthetic biology. Risk scenario of creation of harmful biological agents.
  • 5. Information And Thought Risk: Algorithmic influence on millions. Risk scenario of mass panic, war, and loss of human autonomy.

These are risk scenarios for assessment. They are presented to demand governance, not to create fear.

THE SOLUTION - GUARANTOR

3. The Guarantor Must Be Head Of State - Not Corporation

That is why The Jamil Doctrine says: AI must not be left in the hands of a private corporation.

The Guarantor must be Head of State, under UNO oversight. Because Head of State is responsible to the people. A corporation is responsible only to profit.

This proposal is presented under De Lege Ferenda, law as it should be. It does not assert that a new rule has already been created. It proposes consideration by highest representatives.

THE SOLUTION - TWO INTERNETS

4. Two Internets - To Save Lives

Two Internets, Two Different Governance Systems, One Humanitarian Purpose.

INTERNET 1 - NATIONAL SOVEREIGN INTERNET: The internet of each sovereign state. Each state shall govern its own internet in accordance with its own constitution and laws. No external authority shall replace lawful national control.

INTERNET 2 - UN-NEUTRAL HUMANITARIAN INTERNET: This second internet shall not be under the control of any single state. It shall not be owned or controlled by any single private corporation. It shall operate under neutral United Nations governance. Its sole purpose shall be the protection of human life and saving lives.

Internet 2 is proposed as a logically isolated network with dedicated capacity, not necessarily a fully separate physical network. It is proposed to be supported by neutral satellite infrastructure, so that when cables break and towers fall, communication for hospitals and rescue teams never fails.

THE MESSAGE - FOR THE WORLD

5. We Are Not Against AI - We Are For Safe AI

We are not against AI. We are for safe AI. AI for saving lives, not for destroying lives.

AI should heal the sick, teach the student, help the farmer, save the child in flood and earthquake. AI should not become a bomb that destroys the world.

This is our message to the world. From Abbottabad to UNO. Independent citizen proposal. No claim of official UN endorsement.
