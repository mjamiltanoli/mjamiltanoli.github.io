CHAPTER 8 - THE NEW DAWN

Humanity Will Rise Again

Authored by Muhammad Jamil Tanoli - Abbottabad, Pakistan

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23474749 | Independent Proposal

Chapter 7 was the last warning. Chapter 8 is the new hope.

Every dark night has a morning. Every storm has an end. Humanity has seen wars, hunger, and darkness before. And every time, humanity rose again.

AI is powerful. But AI has no heart. AI has no mother. AI has no tears. AI cannot love a child. AI cannot pray. AI cannot forgive.

Man has all of these. That is why man will always be greater than machine.

The new dawn will come when we use AI for good. AI for hospitals. AI for schools. AI for farmers. AI for poor people. Not AI for weapons. Not AI for control.

I am from Abbottabad. I am a common man. But I believe - if we wake up now, our children will live in a better world.

This is not the end. This is the beginning of a new dawn.
