CHAPTER 6 - THE RISE OF AI

"When Machines Will Rule In 10 Years"

In the next 10 years, Artificial Intelligence will become more powerful than any army, any government, any institution in the world.

Today AI writes. Tomorrow AI will decide. Day after tomorrow AI will control.

It will control jobs. Millions of people will lose work, not because they are lazy, but because a machine will do it faster and cheaper.

It will control war. Drones will kill without a human pressing the button.

It will control truth. AI will make fake videos, fake voices, fake news so real that no one will know what is true and what is false.

And the institutions? They are silent again. Just like in Chapter 5.

If we do not make rules for AI today, in 10 years AI will make rules for us.

Because silence is also a crime. When you know the danger and you stay silent, you are part of the destruction.

Chapter 6 ends here. But the warning continues.

- Muhammad Jamil Tanoli
