Chapter 5 of 10 - The Whistleblower

Jacob Coxon Resigned - And He Was Right

According to his public resignation statement, Jacob Coxon resigned from Anthropic.

PROOF - Public Tweet / X Post:

Jacob Coxon @hilbertspaess
"I resigned from Anthropic today. I spent the last three years doing pretraining research at both OpenAI and Anthropic. Neither company is acting responsibly. They are racing straight to self-improving superintelligence and gambling with our lives."

Date: 09 Sep 2026 - 5:04 AM (Pakistan Time)
Views: 175 Million | Likes: 798K | Reposts: 205K

He stated that he had spent three years doing pretraining research at OpenAI and Anthropic, and in his view, leading labs were moving too fast toward self-improving superintelligence without adequate safeguards.

In his own words, he felt he could not stay silent, and he warned the world about this risk.

"He was inside the system. He saw the danger. He resigned because he could not stay silent. He warned the world."

And Still, The Institutions Stayed Silent

Whether institutions responded adequately remains a matter of public debate. This Doctrine argues that his warning deserves serious, neutral consideration under international law.

Why? Because AI is profitable. AI is powerful. And the institutions that must limit AI are the ones benefiting from it.

This is why this Doctrine exists. Not to attack any individual, but to demand a legal framework where no single company, no single lab, and no single system can gamble with the lives of humanity.

Conclusion

Jacob Coxon's resignation is not just one man's decision. It is a warning. A warning that the world cannot ignore.

This Chapter is presented as a factual record of a public statement, with neutral language, for the consideration of the United Nations, the International Court of Justice, and all humanity.
