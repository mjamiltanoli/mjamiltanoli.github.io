Chapter 5: The Silence of The Institutions

I wrote to them. Not once, but many times.

I wrote to the UN. I wrote to the UNDP. I wrote to UNICEF. I wrote to the ICJ.

I did not write for fame. I did not write for money. I wrote because I saw what is coming.

"Artificial Intelligence, if left without law, without morality, and without human control, can destroy this world within the coming ten years."

And what did they do?

Nothing. Silence.

No reply. No action. No responsibility.

These are the same institutions that were made to protect humanity. To protect children. To protect justice. To protect peace.

But today, when humanity needs them the most, they are silent.

Jacob Coxon Resigned - And He Was Right

Jacob Coxon resigned. He was inside the system. He saw the danger with his own eyes. He saw that AI is growing faster than our laws. Faster than our morals. Faster than our control.

He resigned because he could not stay silent. He warned the world.

And still, the institutions stayed silent.

Why? Because AI is profitable. Because AI is powerful. Because to control AI means to control power - and no one wants to give up power.

The Next Ten Years Will Decide Everything

In the next ten years, AI will decide wars. AI will decide jobs. AI will decide truth and lies. AI will decide who lives and who is forgotten.

If we do not make laws NOW, if we do not make a global human agreement NOW, then in ten years it will be too late.

This is not a technology problem. This is a humanity problem.

I am not a president. I am not a billionaire. I am Jamil. A common man from Muzaffarabad. But I can see what the big institutions cannot see - or do not want to see.

I will keep writing. Chapter after chapter. Page after page. Until 10 pages are complete. Until someone listens.

Because silence is also a crime. When you know the danger and you stay silent, you are part of the destruction.

Chapter 5 ends here. But the warning continues.
