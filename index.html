BOX 7 - SOLUTION - TWO INTERNETS - FULL EXPLANATION

7. Solution: Two Internets + Neutral Satellite Under UNO

Two Internets, Two Different Governance Systems, One Humanitarian Purpose.

The Problem: When war occurs, when an earthquake strikes, or when a flood hits, the first thing that fails is the internet. When the internet fails, doctors cannot reach patients, rescue teams cannot find routes, and families cannot contact each other. Lives are not lost only because help did not exist. Lives are lost because communication did not exist.

The Solution: One internet is not enough. The Jamil Doctrine therefore proposes two internets. These two internets will not replace each other. They will operate under separate governance systems.

  • INTERNET 1 - NATIONAL SOVEREIGN INTERNET: The internet of each sovereign state. Each state shall govern its own internet in accordance with its own constitution and laws. The national government shall remain responsible for its domestic infrastructure, ISPs, cybersecurity, and legal framework. No external authority shall replace lawful national control. This internet includes the State, Military, Banks, Hospitals, Power Grid, Citizens, Business, and Education.
  • INTERNET 2 - UN-NEUTRAL HUMANITARIAN INTERNET: This second internet shall not be under the control of any single state. It shall not be owned or controlled by any single private corporation. It shall operate under neutral United Nations governance. Its sole purpose shall be the protection of human life and saving lives. It shall not become a commercial or political internet.
  • NEUTRAL SATELLITE INFRASTRUCTURE - SUPPORT FOR INTERNET 2: The second internet is proposed to be supported by satellite infrastructure because terrestrial internet depends on cables. Cables can break, towers can fall, and terrestrial networks can be shut down. A satellite operates in space and cannot be shut down by any single state or company at will. Its functions shall include: Connecting areas without terrestrial internet, Maintaining communication during disasters, Supporting emergency medical operations, Providing communication during humanitarian crises, and Connecting rescue organizations.

How It Will Work on a Normal Day: On a normal day, Internet 1 shall operate, and each state shall operate its own internet.

How It Will Work in an Emergency: In an emergency, Internet 2, through satellite, shall operate only for hospitals, rescue teams, and people in need, so that communication never fails.

GUARANTOR: HEAD OF STATE - NOT CORPORATION

Foundational Statement: One Internet belongs to every country's sovereign governance under its own laws. The second Internet belongs to humanity's humanitarian purpose, under neutral United Nations governance, controlled by neither a single country nor a single corporation, and dedicated exclusively to saving human lives.
