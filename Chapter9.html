CHAPTER 9 - THE PROMISE

A World Without Fear

Authored by Muhammad Jamil Tanoli - Abbottabad, Pakistan

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23474749 | Independent Proposal

Chapter 8 gave us a new dawn. Chapter 9 gives us a promise.

I promise my children - and your children - that we will not give them a world of fear.

A world where a child is not afraid of war. A world where a mother is not afraid for her son. A world where a farmer is not afraid of hunger.

AI can help us make this world. If we use it right, no child will sleep hungry. No patient will die because there was no doctor. No student will stay uneducated because there was no teacher.

But this promise needs courage. Courage to say no to weapons. Courage to say no to hate. Courage to say yes to humanity.

I am a common man from Abbottabad. I have no army. I have no money. I have only my pen and my promise.

And my promise is - I will keep writing until the world listens.
