CHAPTER 7 - THE LAST WARNING

Humanity's Final Choice

Authored by Muhammad Jamil Tanoli - Abbottabad, Pakistan

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23474749 | Independent Proposal

Chapter 6 warned you that AI will rule in 10 years. Chapter 7 asks you one question: What will you do now?

We still have time. But not much time.

We have two choices. First choice: We make rules for AI today. We put humanity above profit. We put life above power. We say AI must serve man, not control man.

Second choice: We stay silent. We let companies and governments use AI for weapons, for control, for money. Then in 10 years, there will be no choice left.

The institutions failed in Chapter 5. AI rose in Chapter 6. Now in Chapter 7, the decision is in the hands of common people.

One man from Abbottabad cannot stop AI alone. But one voice can wake up thousands. Thousands can wake up millions.

This is The Jamil Doctrine. I am not a professor. I am not a general. I am a common man who refuses to stay silent.

Because silence is also a crime. When you know the danger and you stay silent, you are part of the destruction.

Chapter 7 ends here. But the warning continues.

I will write one chapter after chapter. Page after page. Until 10 chapters are complete. Until someone listens.
