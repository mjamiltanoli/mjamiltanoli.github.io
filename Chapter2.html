JAMIL DOCTRINE - NAYA CHAPTER

"Jab Idare Khamosh Ho Jayein, To Awam Bolti Hai"

Maine UNO ko likha. Maine UNDP ko likha. Maine UNICEF ko likha. Maine ICJ ko likha. Jawab kya aaya? Khamoshi.

Par ye khamoshi meri haar nahi hai. Ye unki pehchan hai.

Aaj se meri awaz hi mera idara hai. Aaj se meri awam hi meri taqat hai.

Jo khamosh hai, wo zulm ke sath hai. Jo bol raha hai, wo Jamil Doctrine ke sath hai.

- Muhammad Jamil Tanoli
